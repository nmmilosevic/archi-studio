# Reframe Studio · Reply templates (EN)

Paste the text above your signature. Replace everything in `[brackets]`.
Voice: warm, direct, short. One ask per email. Sign off with "Nicolas".

---

## 1. Reply to a new enquiry

**Subject:** Re: your website — next step

Thanks for getting in touch. I had a quick look at [studio name / their current site] and I can already see a few things worth talking about, especially [one specific observation: the project pages, the mobile view, how the portfolio is organised].

The easiest next step is a 20-minute call. You tell me where the studio is heading, I tell you what I'd change first and roughly what it would cost. No pitch deck.

Does [day] at [time] or [day] at [time] work for you? If not, send me two slots that suit you.

Talk soon,
Nicolas
