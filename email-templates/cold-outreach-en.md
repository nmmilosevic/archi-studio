# Reframe Studio · Cold outreach (EN)

First emails to architecture, interior and landscape studios that don't know you yet.

**How to make them work**
- Send them one by one, never as a bulk mailing. Each one starts with something true and specific about *their* studio (a project, their site, their Instagram).
- Keep it under 120 words. One question at the end, easy to answer.
- No attachments, no links in the first email except your website (it lands in spam less).
- Send Tuesday to Thursday, 9:00–11:00 their time.
- Always offer a simple way out ("if it's not for you, just say so and I won't follow up").
- Note: in Spain (LSSI) and the EU, unsolicited commercial emails are restricted. Writing a short, personal, one-to-one message to a studio's public business address, with an easy opt-out, is the safest way to do it. Never buy lists or send bulk.

Replace everything in `[brackets]`.

Best,
Nicolas
Reframe Studio · hello@reframestudio.es

## 1. Main email: the website angle (use this by default)

**Subject:** [Studio name] website
*(alternatives: "A thought on [studio name]'s site" · "Your [project name] project")*

Hi [first name],

I came across [project name] — [one genuine detail you liked: the light in the courtyard, the stone, the way it sits in the landscape]. Beautiful work.

Your website doesn't quite do it justice yet: [one specific, polite observation — the photos are small on mobile / the projects are hard to find / it takes a while to load / there's no Spanish/English version].

I'm Nicolas, I run Reframe Studio. We design websites, brands and print for architects and interior designers on the Costa del Sol, so your work gets the presentation it deserves.

Would it be useful if I sent you three quick ideas for your site? No cost, no strings.

Best,
Nicolas
reframestudio.es

## Quick personalisation checklist (2 minutes per studio)

1. Open their site on your phone. Note the single most visible problem.
2. Pick one project you genuinely like and one detail about it.
3. Find the founder's first name (About page, LinkedIn).
4. Choose the variant that matches what you saw (no site → 2, inconsistent brand → 3, Instagram → 4, otherwise → 1).
5. Write the first line yourself. Never send a template unedited.
