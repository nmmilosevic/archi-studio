# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Architecture, interior design, and landscape studios in Spain and internationally. They are evaluating whether Reframe Studio understands how design-led practices present portfolios, credibility, process, and premium positioning online.

## Product Purpose

Reframe Studio is a specialist web design studio for architecture and design practices. The website should communicate niche expertise in portfolio presentation, website redesign, digital experience, brand perception, search visibility, and credible studio positioning.

## Brand Personality

Precise, architectural, restrained. The voice should feel confident and specialist, with natural English and clear commercial benefits.

## Anti-references

Do not read as a local Costa del Sol agency, a generic web design agency, a keyword-stuffed SEO page, or an exaggerated startup-style marketing site. Avoid repeated city targeting except in project case studies, contact/legal context, or metadata where genuinely relevant.

## Design Principles

- Lead with specialist expertise in architecture and design studios.
- Make portfolio quality, credibility, and search visibility practical rather than abstract.
- Keep copy concise, premium, and human.
- Preserve the existing visual language while improving content clarity.
- Use SEO terms naturally in headings, metadata, alt text, and semantic content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable, direct copy across English, Spanish, and French. Keep navigation labels and calls to action understandable without relying on location-specific assumptions.
